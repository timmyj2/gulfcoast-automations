Services

Every business is different, so every automation is built around your actual workflow — not a one-size-fits-all template. Here's where I typically start.

🔁

Workflow & Process Automation

The repetitive, multi-step tasks your team does manually — approvals, hand-offs, data entry, follow-ups — become automated workflows that run themselves. You define the rules once; the automation handles it every time after.

🧩

Tool Integrations

Your CRM, email, forms, spreadsheets, and other apps probably don't talk to each other yet. I connect them so data moves between them automatically — the right information lands in the right system without anyone copying and pasting it by hand.

📊

Reporting & Data Automation

If someone on your team spends hours building the same report every week or month, I can automate it — pulling data from your existing systems and generating up-to-date reports and dashboards on a schedule, with zero manual work.

🛠️

Custom Scripts & Tools

Sometimes the fix isn't a big platform — it's a small, purpose-built tool. I build lightweight custom scripts and internal tools to solve the specific, oddly-shaped problem that off-the-shelf software doesn't handle.
