Services

Every business is different, so every automation is built around your actual workflow — not a one-size-fits-all template. Here's where we typically start.

🔁

Workflow & Process Automation

We take the repetitive, multi-step tasks your team does manually — approvals, hand-offs, data entry, follow-ups — and turn them into automated workflows that run themselves. You define the rules once; the automation handles it every time after.

🧩

Tool Integrations

Your CRM, email, forms, spreadsheets, and other apps probably don't talk to each other yet. We connect them (using tools like Zapier, Make, or custom scripts) so data moves automatically instead of being copied and pasted by hand.

📊

Reporting & Data Automation

If someone on your team spends hours building the same report every week or month, we can automate it — pulling data from your existing systems and generating up-to-date reports and dashboards on a schedule, with zero manual work.

🛠️

Custom Scripts & Tools

Sometimes the fix isn't a big platform — it's a small, purpose-built tool. We build lightweight custom scripts and internal tools to solve the specific, oddly-shaped problem that off-the-shelf software doesn't handle.
