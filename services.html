Services

Every business is different, so every automation is built around your actual workflow — not a one-size-fits-all template. Here's where I typically start.

💰

Accounting

Invoices go out late, payments get chased by hand, and reconciling the books eats a weekend every month. I automate the parts of that workflow that don't need a person — invoices go out on time, and month-end stops blowing up your weekend.

📊

Reporting

Someone rebuilds the same report from scratch every week or month. I set it up once so the numbers update themselves — the report is ready before anyone has to ask for it.

🧩

Data

Your CRM, invoicing, forms, and spreadsheets don't talk to each other, so someone's stuck copying information between them by hand. I connect them so the right information lands in the right place automatically.

🗓️

Scheduling

Jobs and appointments live on a whiteboard, a group text, or in someone's head — and something always slips through. I build scheduling that runs itself, with reminders that go out without anyone remembering to send them.

📦

Inventory

Nobody's sure what's actually on the shelf until someone walks over and counts it — so you run out of what sells and sit on what doesn't. I set up tracking that stays current without a manual count.

How It Works

A simple, low-risk process

1. Free Automation Game Plan

Up to an hour together going through how your business runs today. Within a week you get it in writing: what's worth automating, what I'd build, and what it costs. No obligation to go further.

2. You Decide

No pressure and nothing to sign up front. If the plan makes sense, we agree on scope and timeline and I get to work. If it doesn't, you keep the plan and we leave it there.

3. Build & Handoff

I build the automation, test it against your real workflow, and make sure your team knows how to use it.

4. Ongoing Support

Optional. A monthly plan keeps your automations maintained as your tools and business change — and gives you someone to bring the next bottleneck to when you spot one.
