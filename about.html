About Gulf Coast Automation

Practical automation for small businesses that don't have time for a manual process that gets in their way.

Why I do this

Small businesses aren't short on effort — they're short on hours. Most of that time gets lost to repetitive work: retyping data between systems, chasing approvals, rebuilding the same report every week. If a process is repeatable, it's automatable — that's what I build.

Who I am

I'm Tim. A decade-plus in corporate accounting, the last five-plus building automation and reporting — now paired with Python and AI.

BS & MS in Accounting MBA 10+ years in corporate accounting Power BI · Power Automate · UiPath · Python

That finance background is the difference: I understand why a process exists and what its numbers mean, not just how to connect the tools. It shows most in invoicing, reconciliation, and month-end reporting — but the same thinking works just as well on scheduling, inventory, or any process nobody's had time to fix.

It's a one-person operation by design. The person who scopes your project is the one who builds it and supports it — no hand-offs.

How I work

I start with your process, not the technology — how work actually moves, where it slows down, where it breaks. Then I build the smallest, most reliable automation that solves it, using tools that fit your budget and your team.

Who it's for

Small businesses and local teams outgrowing spreadsheets and manual work, but not ready for a full software overhaul. If a repetitive process is costing you hours every week, that's exactly the problem I solve.
