About Gulf Coast Automation

Practical automation for small businesses that don't have time for a manual process that gets in their way.

Why Gulf Coast Automation exists

Most small businesses aren't short on effort — they're short on hours. A huge amount of that time gets spent on tasks that are repetitive, easy to describe, and honestly kind of boring: retyping the same information into two systems, chasing down approvals, building the same report from scratch every week.

Gulf Coast Automation started with a simple idea: if a process is repeatable, it's automatable. Instead of asking small businesses to buy expensive enterprise software or hire a full-time developer, I focus on finding the specific, painful bottleneck in your day-to-day operations and building a targeted automation to fix it.

Who's behind this

I'm Tim. I've spent the last decade-plus in accounting for large corporations, and the last five-plus years of that building automation and reporting systems — replacing manual processes with tools like Power BI, Power Automate, and UiPath. More recently I've been pairing AI tools with Python to build automations and reporting in a fraction of the time either approach used to take. I hold a BS and an MS in Accounting, along with an MBA.

That accounting background is the part that actually matters here. Most automation help comes from an IT or software angle — people who know how to wire tools together, but not necessarily why a process exists or what the numbers coming out of it are supposed to mean. Coming from the finance side, I tend to understand the business logic underneath the workflow, so the automation reflects how your books actually need to work instead of just how the software can be connected.

It shows up most in the places manual work quietly costs the most: invoicing, reconciliation, and month-end reporting. Those are the processes I've spent years automating at enterprise scale, and the problems turn out to be remarkably similar at a smaller one — data trapped in the wrong place, reports rebuilt by hand every cycle, a process nobody has had time to fix. The difference is scale, not complexity.

Gulf Coast Automation is also a one-person operation by design. The person who scopes your automation is the person who builds it, and the person you call when you have a question about it later — no account managers, no handing your project to someone you've never spoken to.

My approach

I don't lead with technology — I lead with your process. Every engagement starts by understanding how work actually moves through your business today: who does what, where the hand-offs happen, and where things slow down or break. From there, I design the smallest, most reliable automation that solves the real problem, using tools that fit your budget and your team's comfort level.

The goal isn't to replace your team — it's to give them their time back so they can focus on the parts of the job that actually need a human.

Who I work with

Gulf Coast Automation works with small businesses and local teams who are hitting the limits of spreadsheets, email, and manual processes — but aren't ready for (or don't need) a full software overhaul. If a repetitive process is costing you hours every week, that's exactly the kind of problem I like to solve.
